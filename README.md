#  Dice Multiplier Game

welcome to my latest python project: a dice multiplier game where players roll dice, earn points, try to avoid punishments, and then spend their earnings in the shop at the end of the game!

About the game:
My game is a game that runs off of luck, where players roll 2 dice over 5 game rounds and dice whether to keep rolling for more points or to bank their points before a punishment could occur.

the game includes both rewards and punishments that can make or break your earnings, and at the end of the game, players can spend their points on virtual prizes and view the statistics of their gameplay in the statistics graph when the game ends.

## feautres of the game
- 5 rounds to play
- tkinter welcome screen
- risk vs. reward decisions
- multiple punishments
- score multipliers 
- prize shop system
- roll frequency graph


## punishments
- rolling a 1 on any of the dices resets your round points to 0
- rolling snake eyes (both 1s) resets your round pounts & your entire balance
- rolling 7 halves your round points

## prizes
- players can spend their hard earned points on great vitual prizes such as:
 - candy bar
 - juice box
 - pencil crayon pack
 - phone cases
 - earplug
 - jumbo stuff toy

 ## requirement
 - python 3
 - tkinter (typically included with most python, otherwise install)
